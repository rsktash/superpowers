### (2026-08-19) Decision Beads gains a triage step; two incident parentheticals displaced here.
RULE CHANGE: forks are parked ONLY as decision beads with dep-links, and are triaged under
  precedence (ruling > code > spec > plan-time text) BEFORE being parked. Re-reading an
  inherited fork list re-runs the triage. Also: a gate's factual premises must not contradict
  the spec text they derive from or a standing ruling, not merely "evidence already gathered".

INCIDENT (why the triage step exists): solo-eb4.6.104 carried a prior session's
  "planning-round agenda" of 12 parked design forks, closing with "the epic is not dispatchable
  until every line has a ruling". The epic was dropped from an unattended overnight run on that
  basis. A later session inherited the framing and walked the forks to the owner one at a time.
  Owner: "why the fuck are you being stuck with fucking gates which were written while writing
  plans? isn't the brainstormed plan and decisions made by me is the first priority?" On audit
  exactly ONE was genuinely a decision (which binding an endpoint resolves to when a chat holds
  several counterparties — customer-visible, rulable either way). Eleven were plan defects:
  a gate that never caught up with the rationale three lines above it in the same spec section
  (escalated twice, under two agenda numbers), a bead contradicting itself GET-vs-POST, gate
  vocabulary that never matched the shipped function, a Files-list omission, one answered
  verbatim by a doc comment in the source file, two sequencing errors, two security defaults
  with one safe answer, an unverified third-party API premise wanting an experiment, and four
  gates that merely lacked an owner bead. An agenda line has no owner and no dep-link, so it
  gates nothing mechanically while reading as a blocker — hence "decision bead or nothing".
